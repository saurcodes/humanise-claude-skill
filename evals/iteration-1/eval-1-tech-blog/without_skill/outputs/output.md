3. Cut inflated adjectives — "groundbreaking," "pivotal," "intricate" add weight without adding meaning. Removed or toned them down.
4. Broke up the em-dash construction — The original used a parenthetical aside mid-sentence that felt stiff. Restructured for natural flow.
5. Replaced "underscore" and "showcase" — Common AI verb choices. Replaced with more direct phrasing ("making a genuine case for," "shift the dynamic").
6. Removed "new era of possibilities" — Vague and overused. The rewrite implies forward momentum without stating it explicitly.
