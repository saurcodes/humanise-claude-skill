# Baseline Output — Corporate Memo (No Skill)

## Rewritten Text

Our teams across departments are genuinely excited about the work they're doing, and that energy is showing up in the results. Q3 went well — better than expected in several areas — and it reflects the real effort people have put in. Going forward, we'll keep building on what's working, improve what we deliver to clients, and stay focused on the goals that matter most.

## Changes Made

- Removed hollow openers: "It is important to note that" adds no information and signals AI-generated text; cut entirely.
- Replaced buzzwords with plain language: "fostering a vibrant culture of innovation" becomes "genuinely excited about the work"; "leverage synergies" becomes "build on what's working"; "client-facing deliverables" becomes "what we deliver to clients"; "alignment with strategic objectives" becomes "stay focused on the goals that matter most."
- Made Q3 results concrete and human: "serve as a testament to our commitment to excellence" is vague self-praise — replaced with "went well — better than expected in several areas," which sounds like something a real person would say in a meeting.
- Shortened and tightened: The original packs three ideas into one breathless block of corporate-speak. The rewrite breaks them into natural rhythm without losing meaning.
- Added a human voice: Phrases like "real effort people have put in" and "what's working" feel like a person speaking, not a press release.
